Experimental research tooling. No Pokémon assets are included here.

AnimaEngine was consulted for Nitro formats, decompression, composition and
timeline behavior. Its MIT license and pinned revision are in
ANIMAENGINE-LICENSE.txt.

The controller and multi-cell behavior were also studied against the
pokebw2 decompilation at revision 41f42ca1afe6291f00b0ba0ec3f6099b9ff1346c,
including lib/nnsys/src/g2d/g2d_Animation.c,
lib/nnsys/src/g2d/g2d_MultiCellAnimation.c and src/system/mcss.c. The MCSS
binary parser was written against the structure described by src/system/mcss.c.
The orthographic parent transform and anchor projection study uses
src/system/mcss.c:363-398, 499-510 and 1436-1448. Calculations in the
experimental tool use floating point. Ten active Oshawott quad edge vectors
were compared with matching UVs in one emulator GPU bank within 0.2 pixel per
coordinate. Fixed-point rasterization and general pixel fidelity remain
unverified; RAM and the GPU bank may represent neighboring frames.
Repository: https://github.com/fuddlesworth/pokebw2
The repository identifies its code license as GPL-3.0. This note is not a
determination of the licensing consequences of using these research results.

The test projection matrix was read from a private melonDS 1.1 savestate;
its section and matrix layout were checked against melonDS 1.1 sources
src/Savestate.cpp, src/FIFO.h, src/GPU3D.h and src/GPU3D.cpp. The current
GPU matrix is not established to belong to a particular battle sprite.

In a second private melonDS 1.1 capture, successive paused states had frame
counters 72184 and 72185. The earlier state's camera and Moustillon position
projected a nine-quad prototype pose within 0.403 pixel per axis of all 36
vertices in the later state's render bank. The capture regression retains only
numeric geometry and matrices, not game media or the private savestate. The
source atlas indices and normal palette matched the earlier state's VRAM in
that experiment. A prototype raster approximately matched a JPEG capture after
an empirically fitted screen translation; it is not a lossless pixel comparison
and does not validate blending, the full animation or Player integration.

The project's map_runtime/LICENSE currently contains a placeholder rather than
a license. The consequences of distributing code informed by the GPL source
need to be determined for the intended distribution. AnimaEngine's MIT notice
must be preserved where its license terms require it.
